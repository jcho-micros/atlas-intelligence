# Atlas OS Vision

Atlas OS is an AI Commerce Operating System that discovers market opportunities, converts them into business opportunities, orchestrates specialized agents, and helps the CEO build, launch, and improve profitable product businesses.

## Mission

Help an operator answer one question every day:

> Based on real marketplace data, what business should we build next, why, and what needs to happen to launch it?

## Core Principles

1. The CEO decides. Atlas prepares.
2. Agents own departments.
3. Events are the source of truth.
4. Memory compounds advantage.
5. Businesses own products, products own listings.
6. The dashboard is a window into Atlas OS, not the system itself.

## Long-Term Direction

Atlas should evolve from market intelligence into an autonomous commerce company builder:

Research → Opportunity → Business → Products → Suppliers → Finance → Marketing → Launch → Performance → Learning
