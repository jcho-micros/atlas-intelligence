# Product Requirements Document

## Product Name
Atlas Intelligence

## Mission
Help entrepreneurs discover profitable product opportunities before everyone else using AI, marketplace research, and automation.

## Primary Question
What product should I create and sell next?

## Initial User
John Cho, building an AI-powered commerce research platform focused first on Etsy and later other marketplaces.

## MVP Goals
1. Manage research keywords.
2. Collect marketplace listing data.
3. Store raw listing data in SQLite.
4. Score opportunities with clear reasoning.
5. Display opportunities in a dashboard.
6. Export reports.

## Non-Goals for MVP
- Automated publishing
- Automated ordering or fulfillment
- Mass product generation
- Bypassing marketplace rules

## Success Criteria
Atlas is successful when it can produce a daily ranked list of product opportunities with enough evidence to decide whether to build, watch, or reject each idea.
